### 1.2. Origin of the `XSH-n20-k4` Instances in `09-routing`
Each of the 55 `.vrp` files in QOBLIB (`XSH-n20-k4-01.vrp` to `XSH-n20-k4-55.vrp`) carries the header attribution:
```text
COMMENT : "Queiroga and Uchoa (2024); Optimal cost: ..."
```
and `09-routing/instances/README.md` specifies that the instances were generated by **Eduardo Uchoa** and **Eduardo Queiroga** with 100% tight fleet capacity ($\sum_{i=1}^{20} d_i = 4Q$). Their generator and reference solvers come from the following foundational CVRP papers:

2. **Uchoa, E., Pecin, D., Pessoa, A., Poggi, M., Vidal, T., & Subramanian, A. (2017).**
   - **Title:** *New benchmark instances for the Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem*.
   - **Journal:** **European Journal of Operational Research (EJOR)**, 257(3), 845–858.
   - **DOI:** [10.1016/j.ejor.2016.08.012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejor.2016.08.012)
   - **Role:** Introduces the modern **X-instance** generator (`CVRPLIB`) from which `XSH-n20-k4` (**X**-generator, **S**mall-size $n=20$, **H**ard 100% tight capacity, $k=4$ vehicles) is derived.

3. **Vidal, T. (2022).**
   - **Title:** *Hybrid genetic search for the CVRP: Open-source implementation and SWAP\* neighborhood*.
   - **Journal:** **Computers & Operations Research**, 140, 105643.
   - **DOI:** [10.1016/j.cor.2021.105643](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cor.2021.105643) | **arXiv:** [arXiv:2012.10384](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.10384)
   - **Code:** [github.com/vidalt/HGS-CVRP](https://github.com/vidalt/HGS-CVRP)
   - **Role:** Used by QOBLIB (`09-routing/solutions/README.md`) to compute the reference heuristic solutions (10 seeds, 10s limit).

4. **Erraid, Y., Queiroga, E., Sadykov, R., Uchoa, E., & Vanderbeck, F. (2023).**
   - **Title:** *VRPSolverEasy: a Python library for the exact solution of a rich vehicle routing problem*.
   - **Code:** [github.com/inria-UFF/VRPSolverEasy](https://github.com/inria-UFF/VRPSolverEasy)
   - **Role:** Branch-Cut-and-Price solver used by QOBLIB (`09-routing/solutions/README.md`) to certify optimality on 54 of the 55 reference solutions (`XSH-n20-k4-21` remained uncertified as `.bst.sol` prior to our `TC-Exact` submission).

## 3. History of the `paper/` Directory & Preprint Status (Question A.5 & A.6)

### 3.1. What was in `paper/` (removed on October 18, 2026)?
- The `paper/` folder contained a 4-page LaTeX technical manuscript (`paper/main.tex`, compiled `paper/main.pdf`, and `paper/figures/fig1_sqa_analysis.pdf`) titled:
  > *"Closing the QOBLIB CVRP Benchmark: Exact Optimality Certificates and Subspace Simulated Quantum Annealing for Tight-Capacity Routing"*
- **Why was it removed from `main`?** At the author's explicit request to keep the manuscript draft private while keeping the code repository public for QOBLIB PR #93 verification, `paper/` was removed from the public `main` branch (`git rm -r paper`) and backed up locally outside the git repository (`../paper_local_backup/`).
- **Should it be kept private or re-added?** It should remain **private** (excluded from the public branch) until the author decides whether to submit the manuscript to a journal, conference, or arXiv. However, the figure generator `scripts/plot_figure1.py` remains in the repository so anyone can regenerate `fig1_sqa_analysis.pdf` directly from the experimental JSON data.

### 3.2. Preprint / Publication Submission Status (Question A.6)
- **No external journal or arXiv preprint has been submitted yet.**
- The only external submission made so far is the official benchmark Pull Request **PR #93** ([`ZIB-AOPT/QOBLIB#93`](https://github.com/ZIB-AOPT/QOBLIB/pull/93)) submitting the standalone `TC-Exact` optimal solutions (`20261017_TCExact_Vuong`) to the Zuse Institute Berlin QOBLIB repository.

## 4. Relation to Other Projects & How to Cite (Question G.31 & G.32)

### 4.1. Relation to `BA-VRP` Thesis and `vrp-quantum-hello-world` (Question G.31)
- This repository (`qoblib-routing-exact-sqa`) was developed as an **independent, standalone C++17 research and benchmarking codebase** specifically targeting **QOBLIB Class `09-routing` (`XSH-n20-k4`)**.
- Conceptually, it shares the broader research theme of applying quantum/quantum-inspired optimization to Vehicle Routing Problems (moving from toy/introductory QUBO experiments such as `vrp-quantum-hello-world` or thesis formulations like `BA-VRP` toward rigorous international benchmarks). Its core mathematical contribution—proving that penalty QUBOs fail under zero fleet slack ($\Delta = 0$) and projecting SQA onto the exact capacity-tight set-partitioning manifold—provides a direct bridge between introductory quantum VRP models and competitive exact/quantum-inspired routing solvers.
