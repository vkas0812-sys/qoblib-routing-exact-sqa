## 2. Key Architectural & Mathematical Decisions (Decision Log)

### Decision 1: Exploiting the Zero-Slack Structural Invariant ($\Delta = 0$)
- **Observation:** Across all 55 `.vrp` files generated by Queiroga & Uchoa (2024), total customer demand equals total fleet capacity ($\sum_{i=1}^{20} d_i = 4Q$).
- **Decision:** Project the problem onto the exact single-vehicle subset manifold $\mathcal{F}_1 = \{m \mid \sum_{i \in m} d_i = Q\}$ ($|\mathcal{F}_1| \in [300, 961]$) and the 4-vehicle exact cover manifold $\mathcal{P}_4$ ($|\mathcal{P}_4| \in [372, 5,630]$), evaluating exact route costs via Held-Karp dynamic programming.
- **Outcome:** `TC-Exact` certifies all 55/55 instances in `0.183 s` total (`3.33 ms/instance`), and `SQA-Subspace` ($P=16$ Trotter slices, $T=6.0$, $\Gamma: 35.0 \to 0.175$, $1,000$ sweeps) achieves 55/55 global optimality in `13.2 ms/run`.
