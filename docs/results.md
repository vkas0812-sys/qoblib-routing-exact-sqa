## 2. Explanation of Figure 1 (`fig1_sqa_analysis.pdf`) (Question F.26)

Generated by [`scripts/plot_figure1.py`](../scripts/plot_figure1.py), Figure 1 is a two-panel diagnostic plot illustrating both the quantum-inspired tunneling dynamics and the combinatorial manifold collapse:

### Panel (a): `SQA-Subspace` Tunneling on `XSH-n20-k4-21`
- **What it plots:**
  - **Horizontal axis (log scale):** Monte Carlo sweep index from $1$ to $1,000$ (where $1\text{ sweep} = P = 16$ replica updates).
  - **Vertical axis:** Best-so-far (incumbent) CVRP tour distance across the 5 independent `SQA-Subspace` runs (colored step curves), alongside two horizontal reference lines:
    - Dash-dotted gray line at **`Cost = 843`**: the second-best partition cost $C^{(2)} = 843$, which is also the best solution reached by the prior `MemeticGA` community submission (`ElYaakoubi`, 2026).
    - Solid black line at **`Cost = 842`**: the global optimum $C^* = 842$ certified by `TC-Exact`.
- **Key Conclusion from Panel (a):**
  - Instance `XSH-n20-k4-21` is deceptive because its spectral gap between the global optimum (`842`) and second-best local minimum (`843`) is only $\Delta E = 1$.
  - All 5/5 independent `SQA-Subspace` runs rapidly descend from initial random partitions (`Cost ~ 870`) through intermediate states (`860 -> 847 -> 845 -> 843`) and successfully tunnel across the multi-route barrier into the global optimum `842` (Run #1 hits `842` at sweep 73, i.e., update 1,154 out of 16,000).

### Panel (b): Zero-Slack Manifold Collapse Across All 55 Instances
- **What it plots:**
  - **Horizontal axis:** Instance index $1$ to $55$ (`XSH-n20-k4-01` to `55`).
  - **Vertical axis:** Manifold cardinality showing:
    - Light-blue bars: Number of valid 4-vehicle partitions $\lvert\mathcal{P}_4\rvert \in [372, 5,630]$.
    - Red curve with circular markers: Number of demand-tight single-vehicle subsets $\lvert\mathcal{F}_1\rvert \in [300, 961]$.
- **Key Conclusion from Panel (b):**
  - Enforcing the exact structural invariant $\sum_{i \in S_r} d_i = Q$ collapses the apparent combinatorial explosion of CVRP from $4^{20} \approx 1.1 \times 10^{12}$ assignments (or $2^{4527}$ QUBO states) down to fewer than $1,000$ tight single-vehicle subsets and fewer than $5,630$ feasible 4-vehicle partitions across *every single instance* in QOBLIB `09-routing`.

## 3. Comparison with Best-Known Solutions (BKS) & Optimality Gap (Question F.27)

- **Do all instances reach BKS?**
  - **Yes — 55 / 55 instances (100.0%)** reach the exact Best-Known Solution (BKS) in both `TC-Exact` and `SQA-Subspace`.
- **Average Optimality Gap:**
  - **`0.00%`** across all 55 instances for `TC-Exact` (deterministic exact) and **`0.00%`** for `SQA-Subspace` (5-run best; across all $55 \times 5 = 275$ individual stochastic runs, $266/275 = 96.7\%$ hit the exact optimum `0.00%` gap).
- **Closing the Uncertified Instance `XSH-n20-k4-21`:**
  - In QOBLIB's reference `09-routing/solutions/` directory, 54 instances were marked optimal (`.opt.sol`), while **`XSH-n20-k4-21`** was marked only as best-known (`XSH-n20-k4-21.bst.sol` with `Cost = 842`, status `best known` in `09-routing/solutions/README.md`).
  - Furthermore, the only prior community submission on the QOBLIB leaderboard (`20260721_MemeticGA_LoopUntilFeasible_ElYaakoubi`) achieved `54/55` BKS, getting stuck at `Cost = 843` on `XSH-n20-k4-21` after `3.51 s`.
  - Our solver **strictly improves over `MemeticGA` on `XSH-n20-k4-21` (`842` vs. `843`)** and provides the **first complete optimality certificate (`proven_optimal = TRUE` on 55/55 instances)** in QOBLIB `09-routing`, proving that `Cost = 842` cannot be improved further because no feasible partition with cost $< 842$ exists in $\mathcal{P}_4$.

## 4. Benchmark Hardware & Execution Time Breakdown (Question F.28)

- **Hardware Platform:** Apple Mac with **Apple M4 SoC** (10-core CPU, 16 GB unified memory), running single-threaded (`1 CPU thread`) under `AppleClang 21.0.0` (`-O3 -std=c++17`).
- **Execution Time Summary (All 55 Instances):**
  1. **`TC-Exact`:**
     - **Total time for all 55 instances:** **`0.183 seconds`** (`183.1 ms`).
     - **Mean time per instance:** **`3.33 ms`** (fastest: `0.53 ms` on `XSH-n20-k4-53`; slowest: `14.65 ms` on `XSH-n20-k4-02`).
  2. **`SQA-Subspace` ($P = 16$, $1,000$ sweeps):**
     - **Mean time per single run:** **`13.20 ms`** (including $\mathcal{F}_1/\mathcal{P}_4$ enumeration, $O(|\mathcal{P}_4|^2)$ neighbor graph build, and $16,000$ PIMC replica updates).
     - **Mean Time-to-Solution (TTS):** **`12.77 ms`**.
     - **Total time for 5 runs $\times$ 55 instances = 275 runs:** **`~3.63 seconds`**.
  3. **Full Campaign Script (`./build_campaign` running `TC-Exact` + 5 runs of `SQA-Subspace` across all 55 instances):**
     - Completes end-to-end in **`~0.9–1.5 seconds`** of wall-clock time (since $\mathcal{F}_1$, $\mathcal{P}_4$, and the adjacency graph are shared across the 5 SQA runs per instance).
